- **The slow heel comes down at touchdown, never at lift.** On every slow
  checkpoint the heel-down frames sit in the first third of stance (0.53–
  1.00) and none in the last: the paw lands heel and pad together and the
  heel rises off the floor through stance. sgt-1-120 is the one that also
  keeps it down into mid-stance (0.45 / 0.47), with runs to 4–5 frames.
- **sgt-1 stood flatter with training, away from the target.** Its
  heel-up metatarsus at 700 went from +27 / +46° at 50 to **+12 / +19° at
  120**, the heel-up frames 17–24° short of heel_sched's +36° and the clip's
  +34 / +53°. sgt-1-120 also ran the slow command closest (0.95 m/s against
  1.64 at 50): the heel came down as the policy slowed toward the command.
- **heel_sched does pay against it, weakly.** Contact-gated as built
  (w 6, tent half-width 30°), it pays 0.97–1.21 a step on the slow heel-up
  frames and 0.00–0.03 on heel-down ones, every checkpoint; but at +12°
  against +36° the tent's slope is 6 / 2 / 30 = 0.1 a step a degree per
  leg, and the contrast to the heel-down frames it already loses is ~1 a
  step on 0.12–0.19 of stance frames.

So the slow end's heel-down is the policy's, at touchdown, against both the
clip and heel_sched. The lever, if sgt-2-120 keeps it, is the policy side
(the landing or heel_sched's width), not the generator, and it is for after
sgt-2's rule is read at 120. No term is changed here.
