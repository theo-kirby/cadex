## What sg-s9 shows, and what it does not

- **The best speed-commanded policy so far on tracking and order.** It
  follows the command across 1.83 m/s (1.07 → 2.90), runs rotary on all
  strides at every command (9 / 10 / 10 of 10 seeds), never tied at 1550
  and 2400, with flight on 90 % of strides and every pair over O3's 40 ms
  by the estimate above. 2.90 m/s at 2400 is the fastest SG policy yet
  (sg-s4-50 2.78). `no_tap` passes 8/10 at 2400.
- **It overruns at the slow end** (1.07 at 0.7, 153 %), so 700 is not a
  speed it holds; its slowest held speed is about 1.07 m/s.
- **SG fails.** The slowest is digitigrade on RH only (heel 0.02) but not
  on LH (0.19 > 0.1). The fastest is far under 0.4 (0.24 / 0.12) and U is
  over 0.2 on both legs at 2400 (0.24 / 0.23). The heel does not rise with
  speed: LH 0.19 / 0.26 / 0.24, RH 0.02 / 0.21 / 0.12. The 25 checkpoint's
  rising heel (0.01 → 0.11 / 0.00 → 0.38) did not survive learning to run.
- **RL fails on its third clause only.** Rotary (≥ 8/10) and ≥ 40 ms hold
  at all three commands, but the lag share does not fall with speed: fore
  0.368 / 0.250 / 0.328, hind 0.105 / 0.238 / 0.188.
- **The slow end runs on two hind rhythms**: LH 3.61 Hz against RH 2.00
  (372 / 539 mm strides), the LH tap sg-s4 showed at 1.0 m/s
  (`orun16/hind_rhythm.py`'s finding), with `every_stride` 0/10. The
  slow LH heel share is that leg's extra short contacts.
- **O9 at 80:** pins under 900 °/s at p95 (max p95 749) with no flapping
  swing; cable within 1° (0.46° max); no blow-up; loop gaps 2.8–3.4 mm,
  over 2 mm (the front rods, Q2's deferred item). Every drive reaches peak
  somewhere (margin 0.00); worst RMS 1.70 × rated (`j_spine` at 2400).
- **The spine** moves 24–28° per stride at 1550 and 2400, never at a stop
  (orun7's old spine: ~60 % at its clamp), but only 8.5° at 700.
- The curve has flattened (+0.05 an iteration at the end), so O8's
  "extend only while climbing" argues against a warm extension of this
  task. Next under B1 is the slow reference and the trimmed task, cold.
