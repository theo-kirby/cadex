This project is the copy of ot10's accepted quadruped that you have trained sixteen times, over five sessions, to walk with real steps at a commanded speed. This is a sixth session of the same loop. The behaviour, the frozen success spec, the evaluation seeds and the rules for training are unchanged. The spec is in the script as `walk_spec`, passed to the task as `success=walk_spec` with `goals=[command]`, and you must never loosen, drop or reword a predicate, a seed, a condition or the goal.

WHAT CHANGED IN THE PRODUCT SINCE SESSION 5. Nothing. The engine, the trainer, the exported contact spring (0.004 s, ADR-469) and the rule that a contact margin or gap voids an evaluation (ADR-470) are the ones session 5 ran on. The project is as your last turn left it: the accepted script is your revision 0090, its declared policy is r17-discount-bodyrate, and the operator has changed nothing in it.

THE MECHANISM RULE. Unchanged from session 5. You may change the geometry, the bodies and their collision shapes and contact settings, the joints and their limits, the actuators, and the parameters that set them, through the ordinary edit and accept path, with these bounds:
- it stays a four-legged robot on one free base, and the spec's feet stay the four foot bodies `result["c_foot_fl"]`, `result["c_foot_fr"]`, `result["c_foot_rl"]` and `result["c_foot_rr"]`;
- the floor and its collision plane are not yours to change, and neither is anything that sets the physics for every body (timestep, gravity, solver);
- an actuator stays one the catalog offers, at its catalog limits;
- no contact margin or gap on any shape (`margin_mm` and `gap_mm` stay 0): an evaluation of a model that has one is void.
The spec's thresholds are written in the mechanism's own scale, so three constants follow the mechanism and nothing else: `HIP_MM` and `WEIGHT_N` must equal the accepted model's hip height and weight, which the accepted attempt's task bundle states under `success.scale` (`hip_height_mm`, `weight_n`) and `evaluate` reports under `rig`; and `SPEC_LIFT` keeps its written formula. If a mechanism change moves either value, set the constant to the new value before you train. An evaluation whose `HIP_MM` or `WEIGHT_N` is not its model's is void. A run's reason says what changed and which measurement motivated it. The trainer refuses a warm start across a change to the model or to what the network reads or emits, so after such a change start fresh.

THE RULES FOR TRAINING. Unchanged. Every run is pre-registered by train_start with a wall-clock budget of at most 2400 seconds; for scale, r17's 760 iterations of 2048 environments took about 2,060 s on this model. One run at a time. The training seed is never one of 1101-1110. A run's reason cites a measurement from the previous evaluation: the failing predicate, its measured value and the limit. After each run: store the policy, declare it on the task it was trained on, call evaluate, and read the verdict, the per-seed rows, the metrics, the reward terms, the endings and the filmstrips. A failed evaluation is diagnosed before the next run: which predicate failed, by how much, on which seeds, and what in the task or the mechanism you believe caused it; then say whether the change helped.

WHEN TO STOP. This session is at most three runs. Stop when an evaluation passes on all ten seeds, or when this session has trained and evaluated three runs, whichever comes first. A start the trainer refuses before its first iteration is not a run. Do not stop after a failed evaluation while runs remain. End with a short report: each run of this session, what it changed in the task or the mechanism and why, its evaluation, and what you would change next.
