**The rule at 50** (`sg-task.md`, written before judging): stop if the
2400 command's heel share is not above the 1000 command's on either leg
(it is above on both), **or the 2400 command runs under 1.9 m/s (0.26:
fires)**. sg-s1 was stopped at 50 (`loop.request_stop`, 09:54), a dead
end for speed.

## Why it steps in place: the speed kernel

The gallop task pays speed linearly up to `sprint_speed`; sg-s1's
`track_speed` (w 20) paid a Laplace kernel, `exp(-|v - cmd| / (0.2 cmd))`.
At a standstill that is exp(−5) ≈ 0.007 of its weight, with a slope of
0.034 / cmd, a thirtieth of a linear ramp's 1 / cmd. Tracking the pose,
lift and contact of the reference pays the same at any forward speed,
so nothing pulled the policy forward. This is cat-bound h1's trap (the
script's own note: a narrow kernel "paid nothing at the 1.45 m/s an
episode starts at, so the policy bounded on the spot").

Scored per step on sg-s1-25's traces (`tent_term.py`, the chest's speed
frame to frame, first second dropped), each as a share of the weight:

| evaluation | median v mm/s | kernel | tent |
|---|---|---|---|
| sg-s1-25-1000 | 41 | 0.037 | −0.020 |
| sg-s1-25-1700 | −26 | 0.015 | −0.008 |
| sg-s1-25-2400 | −76 | 0.010 | −0.018 |

The kernel paid the drift a little; the tent pays it nothing (and takes
some back for drifting backwards), and pays every mm/s forward 1 / cmd.

## sg-s2: one lever, the speed term

Leo revision **`32dd914b`** (project commit `8e5bb8e`, `cadex script
--set orun16-sg2.py`). The only change, in the `gallop_sg` task:

```
track_speed = UP * (comv_x - 0.75 * (comv_x - cmd + abs(comv_x - cmd))) / cmd
```

A tent: `v / cmd` up to the command (full weight at it, as the kernel),
then falling at half that slope past it, so the slow commands are not
overrun for free. Weight, reference strides, gate, goal and the other 20
terms are unchanged: sg-s2's `gallop_sg-task.json` against sg-s1's
differs in `reward[1].expression` and nothing else (checked key by key),
and the model is the same. The gallop task and its bundle are untouched.

**Launched** by `orun13/queue_run.py ... sg-s2 9000 80 ... --seed 1
--task gallop_sg --envs 4096 --after sg-s1 --build orun16-sg2.py`, in its
own `systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=30G` (`~/orun16-queue/sg-s2.log`):
built 09:33:55, queued behind sg-s1, **launched 09:54:59** when sg-s1
stopped. Cold, same seed as sg-s1 so the speed term is the difference.
Budget as sg-s1's (80 × ~70–85 s + 20 % + compile).
**Timed:** 580 s of trainer wall to iteration 5 (compile included), the
trainer's ETA 7,150 s more at 10:05, so 80 iterations end near 12:05,

`073ff4ad`.

**Rules, written before judging** (at 25 and 50, commands 1000, 1700,
2400, with `sg_judge.py`):

- at 25: stop if the 2400 command's median top 2 s speed is under
  1.0 m/s (sg-s1-25: 0.46), i.e. the term has not moved its measure;
- at 50: stop and change one lever if the 2400 command runs under
  1.9 m/s, or the heel share at 2400 is not above 1000's on either leg;
- report pair lag in ms at every command with strides.
