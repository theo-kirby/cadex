## The shape at 50: not there

O8's shape is the heel rising with speed, rotary at the slow end, and
tracking the command. At 50 none of the three holds:
- **Tracking:** 1.64 m/s at a 0.7 command (234 %); a span of 1.10 m/s
  (sg-s9-50: 0.57; sg-s9-80: 1.83). It is faster than sg-s9-50 at 1550
  and 2400 (2.33 / 2.74 against 1.93 / 2.25).
- **Rotary slow:** the fores land together at 700 (0.39 of strides, 20 ms
  median), as sg-s9-50's did (0.041 share, "fores together 0.57"), which
  sg-s9 lost by 80 (0.368, 180 ms).
- **Heel:** low everywhere (0.04–0.16) and flat on LH.

So 50 is where sg-s9 was at 50, faster but with the same slow-end faults;
its long-run plan waits for 120. No warm long run is planned yet.

## Why: LH lands reaching, heel first, the paw folded

The two hind legs run different feet at every command (O5):
- **LH** lands ahead of the fore track (overreach +121…+214 mm against
  RH's −295…−344), on its heel with the paw toes-up of the floor: U 0.35
  → 0.60 rising with speed, floor pitch in stance +16° at 2400, pin +32°,
  on the pad (R) for only 0.35 of on-ground frames at 2400. Its engine
  heel share (0.08–0.16) is that heel, not a flat foot.
- **RH** stands on the toe bulbs (T 0.21 → 0.44 rising with speed), heel
  24–39 mm up, U 0.07–0.09, lifting toes-down of the floor at −17…−23°.

Against sg-s9-50 (which carried `paw_fold` w −10 and `hind_overreach`):
LH's U is worse at every command (0.36 / 0.35 / 0.60 against 0.22 / 0.27
/ 0.42), RH's is no worse (0.07 / 0.08 / 0.09 against 0.05 / 0.13 / 0.13).
Two of the cut terms bear directly on that leg: `paw_fold` pays against
the paw toes-up of the floor in stance and `hind_overreach` pays the hinds
landing behind the fores. orun15 found the spring rate, not `paw_fold`,
took the fold out on the single-speed task; at 50 on the speed-commanded
one the fold on LH is back past sg-s9-50's. That is one checkpoint and
not the keep rule's: the rule's U clause (≤ 0.24 / 0.23 at 2400) is read
at 120. If LH's U at 2400 is still over 0.24 there, the trim fails it,
and the first term back (O8, one at a time, judged) is `paw_fold`.

## Open for the owner: U at 5° or at 10°

The charter defines U as the paw toes-up of the floor "past 5°" and
cites `paw_floor.py`, whose threshold is 10°. Every U judged since orun14
(hk12-s1-50's 0.16 / 0.01, P1's reference) is the 10° one, and the
verdicts here stay on it so numbers compare across runs. Both are
printed: at 2400, sgt-1-50 is 0.60 / 0.09 at 10° and 0.68 / 0.13 at 5°;
sg-s9-80 0.24 / 0.23 and 0.32 / 0.30. The choice decides P1 for a policy
between the two (hk12-s1-50 would need re-reading at 5°).
