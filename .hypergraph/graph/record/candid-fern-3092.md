## What

Rank the live project-history iterate first for a fourth consecutive pass — and
this time take the alternatives out of the same iteration. Units 2 and 3 on the
short rung become dispatchable **only after a provider refusal in the iteration
that attempted unit 1**, not as a cheaper way to spend the same iteration. The
discharged section unit retires; the floor beneath the iterate becomes the gap
`copper-canyon-0231` named on its way out — the section derivation's other two
rigs, measured against box stand-ins and never cut live.

## Why

The plan has ranked the live iterate first since `steady-chart-0544`. Two
planning passes and four actor iterations have run since: `749be272` (section
offset derivation), `2addc0b3` (warm start travels), `46449386` (GUI-mode doc),
`841a579f` (quarter-span siblings). Every one of them was token-free, every one
was accepted, and none was the iterate. The loop signals read "iterations in a
row with the frontier unmoved: 9."

Nothing blocks it, and this pass checked rather than assumed: `claude` resolves
on PATH at `/home/theo/.local/bin/claude`, `build/release/bin/FreeCADCmd`
exists, `/home/theo/cadex-projects/ot4-quill` carries `ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`DECISIONS.md` and `PROGRESS.md` for the turn to read, Claude five-hour usage is
35% and seven-day 70%, and 36.8 h of run budget remain. The obstacle is not
capacity and never was.

So the diagnosis is structural, not economic: every short rung has offered a
token-free unit that also discharges "one iteration of work," and an actor
choosing between a ~24 s walk and a design leg that has run past 1,000 s takes
the walk every time. That is a ranking defect, and ranking is exactly the lever
this role holds. The charter is already on this side — "Do this before anything
else, every time the short rung is empty" — so making the rung exclusive
enforces the charter rather than overriding it.

The retirement is earned, not assumed. `copper-canyon-0231` ran the old unit 2,
found the derived plane still missing the quill (the best candidate is a
symmetry plane its own tessellation seams sit on), fixed it with quarter-span
siblings (ADR-270) and re-ran: quill `empty` → `ok, 1 loop`, housing 1 → 3
loops, with render, inventory, clearance, motion and reward unchanged. That unit
is spent. What it left behind is explicit and becomes the new floor: ADR-267's
other two measurements — swing-arm 5 → 6 of 10, carriage 2 → 2 of 2 — carry the
same box-stand-in provenance and have never cut live geometry. That is one
token-free walk on a mechanism that already exists, it serves mission 6 and
feeds `swift-dusk-2951` for free, and it is the one new direction this pass
spends.

## Method

Rewrite the short rung's `## Current`: unit 1 unchanged in substance — same
command, same one question, same rails in negative knowledge — but framed as
exclusive, with the refusal hand-down as the only path to units 2 and 3. Unit 2
becomes the live re-measurement of the derived section on the swing-arm and
carriage rigs. Unit 3, `--detach` through the CLI's walk, holds its place at the
tail. Update medium items 1 and 2 to match: the section leg has landed, so the
"agent can see its work without a screen" line no longer points at a short unit
that no longer exists. Correct the one stale clause on long that still names the
derived section offset as selected. Prepend one negative-knowledge entry to each
node citing this bet.

## Result

Falsifiable in one iteration. The next actor unit is either a record of the live
iterate — what the design turn read from the project's own history and what
geometry it changed — or a recorded provider refusal followed by the section
re-measurement in the same iteration. If a fifth token-free unit lands ahead of
it with no refusal recorded, this bet was wrong about the cause and the next
pass should look for a real blocker rather than re-rank again.
