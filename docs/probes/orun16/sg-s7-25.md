**Read.**
- **The contact gate evened the legs and turned RH's schedule, but
  compressed it.** Both legs now rise with the command (LH 0.20 → 0.23 →
  0.25, RH 0.19 → 0.32 → 0.31) and stand within 5% of each other at 1000,
  where sg-s6-25 had RH at twice LH. But the slow heel rose on LH
  (0.14 → 0.20) and the fast heel fell on RH (0.42 → 0.31): a 0.05 / 0.12
  rise from 1000 to 2400 against the 0.15 SG's 50 rule asks.
- **The slow heel is down in early contact, where the term already pays it
  up.** At 1000, 0.97 of LH's and 0.79 of RH's heel-down frames fall before
  the clip's touchdown (the paw lands about 0.1 of a stride early, phase
  tenth 9 in `hind_rhythm.py`), and the contact-gated `heel_sched` pays
  those frames 1.3 less than the heel-up ones. The heel still lands first:
  whatever puts it down at the slow end is not a stance reward.
- **RL's slow end is fixed, unexpectedly.** LH's mid-swing taps fell
  0.64 → 0.38 a stride (`tap-lever.md` predicted the contact gate would
  make them slightly cheaper), and the 1000 hind lag, taps counted, went
  from 30 ms to **140 ms** (0.280 of the stride; worst seed 95 ms). At
  sg-s7-25, RL's 50 rule holds on every clause: rotary 10/10 at every
  command, every pair's median lag ≥ 59 ms (worst seed 42 ms), and both
  lag shares fall from 1000 to 2400 (fore 0.240 → 0.200 → 0.188, hind
  0.280 → 0.250 → 0.176). RL is not ticked: it is judged on the SG
  policy, and SG is open.
- **So `hind_scuff` at w −16 is not the next screen.** The critic's
  condition for it was a stop on the taps; this stop is on the heel, and
  the taps no longer cost the hind lag.

**The heel lever to drop.** Five screens (sg-s3 to sg-s7) have reshaped the
stance heel reward (`heel_tail`, `heel_sched` gated on the clip, then on
contact) and the heel's stance share has stayed between 0.05 and 0.42 at
every command, with each change moving one leg or one end at the other's
cost. The stance-reward lever is spent: the next heel step should not be
another heel term. The slow heel is down in early contact, before the
clip's touchdown, so the next measurement is the reference itself at the
slow end: the clip's metatarsus pitch over its late swing at sg0 (heels up,
`trail_feet` holding the paw flat), the pose `track_pose` and `track_lift`
pay the paw into early. If the clip lands the slow paw heel-flat, the
schedule belongs in the clip's late swing, not a reward. `heel_sched` stays
in the task; nothing is added until that is measured.
