Verified against source: 2026-10-23. Frozen evidence (orun18, iteration 4).
The midpoint judgement D7 asks for, on D4's speed-only task (`b1-task.md`).
A cold screen at 50 is a midpoint: orun17's SG policies did not track


**The judge.** `speed_judge.py` is `orun16/sg_judge.py` for the `speed`
bundle and `speed_spec`: the held command is swapped per judged command in
the judged copy only, on C2_SEEDS 1001–1010, by the engine's evaluation.
The engine reads footfall (pair lags, touchdown rates, overreach) only
against a reference's leg labels, and this task has none, so the judge
fills each seed's `detail.footfall` afterwards with the engine's own
`CadexEvaluation.footfall_metrics`, given leo's four paws as labels, and
marks it `footfall_source`. Then `sg_tables.py` reads B1 as it read
orun17. Checked first on checkpoint 25 (2 seeds, 1500 mm/s): that policy
stands still and tips at 7.6 s; the tables read it without error.

**What it does.** It ignores the command: the same ~1.3–1.45 m/s lope at
every command, a little slower as the command rises (the top 2 s median
falls 1.45 → 1.30). It runs **nose-down**: on seed 1001 the chest frame's
forward axis pitches a median 25° (700) and 26° (5000) below level from 1 s
on, with the chest 30–38 mm below its start. That puts it on the `tipped`
termination's edge (up < 0.85, 31.8°) on every seed, so `upright` (30°)
fails on 1–7 seeds a command. The fores carry it: every drive over its
rating is a fore drive or the spine (below). The hind paws never put a heel
down (heel 0.00, the heel 72–73 mm up in stance), and `paw_floor` reads the
floor pitch at lift at −89 to −108° (sgt-4-50: −1 to −14°): they leave the
ground pointed down, on their toes. The footfall is a short-flight lope at ~3–3.5 Hz,
rotary on 1–6 seeds of 10 and turning less rotary as the command rises, as
the hinds come to land together (0.10 → 0.29 of strides).

## Read at the midpoint

- **Speed only keeps it up.** On D4's task leo stays up on 50 of 50 seeds at
  50 iterations (sgt-4 at 50: 29 of 30, `../orun17/sgt-4-50.md`). It has not
  learned the command: it settles on one speed, ~1.4 m/s, below the range's
  middle (2.85 m/s).
- **The fores bind first.** Shoulder, elbow and wrist run at 1.4–1.95× their
  rated RMS and at their clamp on 0.1–0.26 of frames; the hinds' drives sit
  at 0.34–0.85 of theirs. A nose-down posture that hangs the chest on the
  fore drives is what a cold policy found cheapest; whether it lifts as the
  command is learned is the end judgement's to say.
- **The end judgement at 180** reads the same tables at the same five
  commands, and gives B1's top speed for B2.
