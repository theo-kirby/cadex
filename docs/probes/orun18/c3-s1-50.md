**The judge.** `speed_judge.py` now takes `--task` (default `speed`, B1's).
The C3 bundle's `speed_spec` is B1's with `cmd` narrowed to the strides'
span, 2756–4233 mm/s, so the judged commands are the three strides' speeds
and one blend (3800). That bundle carries a reference whose legs are leo's
four paws, so the engine reads its footfall itself and the judge's
`add_footfall` is not applied. Same C2_SEEDS 1001–1010, same engine
evaluation. Nothing slower than 2756 can be commanded: C3's slow end is the
dog's floor (`c3-task.md`), so sgt-4-120's 700 and 1550 columns have no C3
twin.

**sg_tables at 2756.** Seed 1007 tipped at 0.44 s (`terminations`
`{"horizon": 9, "tipped": 1}`; the engine's `falls` reads 0 because the
episode ended before its settle). Every table reads from the settle (1 s)
on, so `sg_tables.py` and `b1_summary.py` stop on that seed's empty series.
The tables for 2756 are therefore read over the nine seeds that stood
(`c3-s1-50-2756-up`, the same files hard-linked, the one seed dropped and
the drop written into its `evaluation.json` as `derived`). **That seed is
a fall**, and is counted as one below.

## Read at the midpoint, beside B1 and sgt-4-120

- **The clip moves the gait.** At its midpoint C3 gallops: rotary on 39 of
  39 standing seeds, flight on 0.89–1.00 of strides for 0.08–0.12 s, a
  long stride (2.3–2.4 Hz). B1 at 50 loped at 3.0–3.5 Hz with 0.04 s of
  flight, rotary on 1–6 seeds of 10 (`b1-s2-50.md`), and at 180 trotted
  and half-bounded (`b1-s2-180.md`). sgt-4-120 at 2400 was rotary 10/10
  with 0.04 s flight at 3.0 Hz.
- **The clip does not move the speed, yet.** Settled 1.6–1.9 m/s at
  2756–4233 commanded, against b1-s2-180's 1.80 at 3500 and 1.62 at 5000,
  and sgt-4-120's 2.66 at 2400 (its 2 s top; it tracked its command).
  None of the three reaches the dog's slowest gallop on leo, 2.76 m/s.
- **The load moves off the shoulder onto the wrist.** B1's worst drive was
  the shoulder (1.83–1.95 at 50, 1.39–1.65 at 180); C3's is the wrist
  (1.43–1.57), shoulder 0.60–1.00. The fore drives still bind first; no
  hind drive reaches 1.25.
- **A fall on 1 of 40**, at the slowest command, at the reset (0.44 s):
  the dog's slowest stride is the one a cold policy holds least.
- The 50 is a midpoint: reward per step is rising (15.4), as B1's was at
  50. The end judgement (150) reads whether the settled speed rises to the
  command, and runs the mirror check on the left/right split.
