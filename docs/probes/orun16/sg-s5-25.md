**Read.**
- **`heel_sched` schedules LH, not RH.** LH: 0.06 → 0.18 → 0.26, the
  digitigrade-to-flatter shape SG asks for (short of P1's 0.4). RH went the
  other way, and its slow heel came down further than under `heel_tail`.
- **The term pays RH to lift its heel at 1.0 m/s and RH did not.**
  `heel_sched.py` on these traces, at 1000: RH heel-up frames earn 2.47 a
  frame (× w), heel-down ones 0.02, a contrast of +2.45 with 0.2 a degree
  of slope (RH's heel-up metatarsus sits at +18.8°, its target +36°). The
  same contrast as on LH (+2.44). So it is not the term's sign or scale. The
  probe uses paw-down frames for the clip's stance flag. RH lands 0.15 of a
  stride before the clip's touchdown (`hind_rhythm.py`: 0.85 of RH's
  touchdowns in the clip's ninth tenth before it), where `ref_rh_down` is 0
  and the term pays nothing. If RH's heel-down frames fall in that early
  contact, `heel_sched` never sees them. This is the next thing to measure,
  not shown here.
- **Speed fell a little at every command** (1.11 / 2.02 / 2.65 against
  1.19 / 2.13 / 2.78), still inside the 30% band.
- **LH's tap persists** (0.88 a stride at 1000, 0.99 under sg-s4-50) and is
  the whole of the 1000 hind "tie": without it the hind lag falls with the
  command, 0.360 → 0.255 → 0.180, RL's direction. Fore lag falls too,
  0.240 → 0.211 → 0.176.
- O9 on these traces: pin p95 under 900 °/s at every command (worst 866),

## sg-s6, built and launched

`hind_scuff` (`sg-s4-50.md`, scored there per step: 0.99 of its charge on
LH's taps, about zero at 1.7 and 2.4 m/s) on sg-s5's task, w −8:

```
(chl_down * sat01((ref_lh_lift - 10) / 20) + chr_down * sat01((ref_rh_lift - 10) / 20)) / 2
sat01(x) = (x + |x|)/2 - (x - 1 + |x - 1|)/2
```

`~/orun16-queue/orun16-sg6.py` is `orun16-sg5.py` plus that one term (24
terms, cap 32). Built with `cadex script --set` (reward only, so the model
and its clearance are unchanged); no tag (O1 tags model changes only). Warm
from **sg-s5-25** (`7b7524b6`) across the one-term task change, critic
warm-up 5, lr 1e-4, seed 1, 4096 envs, 50 iterations, a checkpoint every
25, `budget_s` 5,000 (50 × 65 s plus 20% plus compile). The build's
revision, the launch time and the log are in `~/orun16-queue/sg-s6.log`.

It keeps `heel_sched`: LH's schedule moved under it in 25 iterations, and
RH's failure has a candidate cause (early landings outside the clip's
stance) that a tap term may also move, since RH's early landing and LH's
tap fall together.

**The question:** does `hind_scuff` take LH's mid-swing tap out at
1.0 m/s, so the hind pair reads rotary with real lag at the slow end?

**Rule at 25** (the same commands and probes as above): run on to 50 only
if, at 1000, LH's taps a clip stride (`hind_rhythm.py`) are at most 0.3 and
`p2_lag`'s hind lag is at least 40 ms (taps counted), with rotary 8/10 at
every command and 2400's median top 2 s speed at least 2.4 m/s. Otherwise
stop it as a dead end and record which clause failed. Report the heel per
leg at every command either way.

**Rule at 50:** RL's direction holds if, at every command, rotary is 8/10
with each pair's lag at least 40 ms (taps counted) and the fore and hind lag
shares fall from 1000 to 2400; SG's schedule moves if both legs' heel share
rises from 1000 to 2400 by at least 0.15, with 1000 at most 0.1. Extend
warm (O8) only if both hold.
