**What it does.** It still does not track the command, but it no longer
holds one speed (50: ~1.4 m/s at every command). At 700 and 1500 it trots
(diagonals within 0.1–0.3 of a stride, 5 Hz, short flight), at 1.84 and
**2.59 m/s**, chest up (tilt 12–16°, against 30° at 50). At 2400 and above
it accelerates to the same ~2.6 m/s for 1–2 s, then by 2–3 s switches to a
**half-bound** (hinds together, fores 0.2–0.37 of a stride later, ~3.2 Hz,
spine 27–29° a stride, tilt 28°) and settles **slower**: 1.85, 1.80 and 1.62
m/s, falling as the command rises. The command is learned in direction only
below 1.5 m/s (it overruns 700 by 2.6×); above, a higher command buys a
different gait, not more speed. The hind paws never put a heel down (heel
stance 70–74 mm up) and leave the ground toe-down (−87 to −110°).

**The fastest it holds** is the trot at command 1500: **2.59 m/s** over the
last 2 s (min seed 2.53), 2.62 m/s at best on any 2 s window. The highest
2 s window on any seed at any command is 2.71 m/s (2400, before the switch).

## Read at the end

- **Speed alone trains a stable runner, not a command follower, in 180
  iterations.** 0 falls on 50 seeds, upright on 50 of 50, but the settled
  speed is 1.6–2.6 m/s whatever the command, with the fastest at the
  1.5 m/s command. The reward is still climbing at 179.
- **The trot is the fast gait; the half-bound is a slower one it picks at
  high commands.** Both start at ~2.6 m/s; only the trot holds it. Nothing
  in D4's task names a gait, so this is the body's and the reward's choice.
- **The fores bind.** The scapula and shoulder run 1.2–1.8× their rated RMS
  at every command, at the peak (margin 0.00), and the shoulder and elbow
  are at their clamp on 0.05–0.17 of frames; the hinds' hip and hock stay
  at 0.2–0.85 of rated, the knee about 1.0. `j_tail2` sits at its clamp on
  0.28–0.49 of frames.
- **No heel, no U, by itself.** Without heel terms the hind paw is
  digitigrade at every speed (heel 0.00, U 0.00).
