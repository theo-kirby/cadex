**Not judged at 4.63 or 5.0 m/s.** The critic asked for both. The bundle's
`cmd` goal spans the dog strides' 2755.5–4233.4 mm/s, and `speed_judge.py`
refuses a command outside the goal's trained range: the reference blends
strides only inside it, so a command above 4.23 is not a task this policy
was trained on. Above 4.23 the comparison is s2a-hold-150's own table.

## What the extra 250 iterations bought

- **Bought:** a third tracked command (4.23), seed 1007 caught, the hind
  feet on every stride of the clip, the clip's stride frequency kept at
  4.23, the right fore on the clip at 2.76, 0.2–0.3× less torque on the
  worst drive, and the loops back under 2 mm.
- **Cost:** speed. It holds 2.93 / 3.73 / 4.43 m/s where m2-dog-150 held
  3.67 / 4.36 / 4.83. The reference terms pull the policy toward the clip,
  and the clip's speed is the command. So more iterations on the dog's
  bundle give a truer dog gallop, not a faster leo.
- **Not bought:** the left fore still double-taps, and the heel pad is still
  never used.

The owner ranks faster higher. On that ranking L1 reads below m2-dog-150 on
held speed and below s2a-hold-150 on the top tracked speed (4.43 against
4.57). On following the motion and on torque it reads best of the three.
