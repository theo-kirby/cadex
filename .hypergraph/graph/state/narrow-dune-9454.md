## Negative knowledge

- [scope: dispatching F6's frozen Robin prompts while `claude-fable-5` is refused at the organisation level | confidence: high | evidence: idle-crow-9434, mellow-sky-2112] Waiting for a five-hour reset does not unblock it. The five-hour window read 1–20 % across eight probes that were all refused, because `seven_day_overage_included` sat at 100 % with `overageDisabledReason: org_level_disabled`. Check with `run.py window`, which spends no slot and since ADR-364 reports the refusal and its cause, rather than spending a frozen prompt to find out.
- [scope: reading the rejected window's `resets_at` (2026-09-18T14:00:00Z) as evidence about when the refusal lifts, in either direction | confidence: high | evidence: soft-journey-2954, mellow-marsh-0749, sage-isle-3511, modest-banner-8771] It is neither a dated opening nor a floor. A scheduled usage-window reset says nothing about `overage.disabled_reason: "org_level_disabled"`, an organisation-level setting that can change at any time — and it did: the owner's restore lifted the refusal on 2026-09-17, well before that reset. Only an unrefused probe shows the block cleared.
- [scope: trusting a refusal receipt written before ADR-369 to name which limit refused | confidence: high | evidence: mellow-marsh-0749] It named whichever `rate_limit_event` frame the provider sent first, which at 17:02 UTC was an *allowed* five-hour frame beside `room: false`, dropping the 100 % overage window that was the cause. Refused probes now read the rejecting frame.
- [scope: dispatching a frozen design turn from a collector whose parent session may exit before the runner does | confidence: high | evidence: modest-dune-5265, weathered-fountain-7838] A dead parent kills the collector mid-stream and the receipt sticks at `running` with no result frame and no exit code — `ot7-robin-b` was lost exactly this way. Hold the dispatching session alive until the runner exits. The inverse also happens and is cheaper than it looks: iteration 160 died at its own actor bound *after* `continue-1` had ended on its own, so the turn was complete and unrecorded, recoverable by verifying the retained stream rather than the receipt row.
- [scope: switching the product agent's model between turns of an ot7 attempt, before ADR-384 | confidence: high | evidence: steady-sail-9171] Changing the loop's configured model does not move the next design turn: `run.py resume` ignored `--model` and reused the attempt receipt's model. Resume now honours an explicit override and records the transition; omitting `--model` still preserves the receipt model, and the attempt is reported as mixed-model rather than as a benchmark of either.
- [scope: an ot7 frozen turn whose child exited before any provider frame was written | confidence: high | evidence: terse-dew-6200] A nonzero exit is not evidence a model was reached. Robin's `continue-1` was refused by the CLI's own project-open guard in six seconds with no transcript, and the runner booked it as a spent slot and closed the design. Read the pair of facts together (no provider frames *and* an envelope error with an empty `session_id`) before deciding a slot was spent, and retry an unreached call in place.
- [scope: a design whose only moving joints are free-spinning wheels | confidence: high | evidence: weathered-fountain-7838] "The sweep has nothing to check here" is not the only reading available, and it was not the right one. Robin's create turn left both axles limit-free and the sweep `incomplete`; `continue-1` found the interface that was never checked — a keyed D-bore on a catalog D-shaft drawn at one fixed angle drives material through the shaft at every angle but zero — and fixed the design rather than the declaration, with a split clamp hub bored on the shaft's envelope of revolution. The limit-free row was a real coverage hole, which is ADR-375's own claim confirmed on the design it was written for.
