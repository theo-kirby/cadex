**`s1_tables.py` (new)** replaces `orun17/sg_tables.py` for v06: that one
reads the hind paw on its pin (`orun14/paw_floor.py`), and v06 has no pin.
It reads only `evaluation.json`: footfall phases, flight, the heel pad, the
drives at their clamp and the closure (its docstring has the definitions).
The **heel pad's share of hind stance** is the engine's `heel_down_share`:
the task's `success.heels` are `c_heel_hl/hr`, the pads behind the hock, so
it is the share of the ball's settled stance frames with the pad within
`STANCE_MM` of the floor. `b1_summary.py` is unchanged; its `wrist` row
reads `nan` on v06 (no wrist drive), and `j_tail2` has no rating.
