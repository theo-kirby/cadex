- **On held speed and torque the dog fits better.** On 3.5, the one command
  it shares with S2's table, it holds 4.36 against s2a-hold's 4.08. Its speed
  follows the command, its top (4.83) is 0.46 m/s past M1's,
  and its worst drive is under M1's spine at 2.76–3.5.
- **On following the clip the cheetah fits better.** M1 keeps the clip's
  order and stride at every command with the hind feet on every stride. M2
  keeps them only up to 3.5, its fore feet miss most strides, and at 4.23 it
  drops the clip's stride for a faster one of its own.
- So the dog's strides, slower than v06 can run, act as a start more than a
  target: v06 runs them 1.1–1.3× long and fast. The cheetah's strides hold
  v06 to their frequency.

## Read

M2 has its judgement beside M1, S2 and v05's c3-s1-50. Trained to 150, v06 on
the dog's strides is the fastest v06 policy so far on a tracked command (4.36
m/s at 3.5, Fr 4.89) and holds 4.83 m/s on nine of ten seeds at 4.23. Its
fore feet double-tap like M1's, and more often. At 50 it ran no faster than
v05's c3-s1-50. Against s2a-hold, it tracks fewer commands, because its
bundle reaches no lower than 2.76 m/s, and it lost one seed at 4.23 at reset.
