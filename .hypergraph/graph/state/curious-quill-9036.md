## Current

**The product publishes opt-in, bounded exact-solid sweeps for limited rigid-tree hinges and sliders (ADR-349, ADR-351).** `assembly.assembly(..., sweep_step_degrees=, sweep_step_mm=)` produces `clearance_sweep` before simulation moves components. Fresh sandbox-inheriting FreeCAD subprocesses move copied descendant BREPs from solved connector frames, preserving other joint coordinates and parent placements. Each swept joint names its `kind`, `unit` and `step` and reports `range_<unit>`, `initial_<unit>` and `first_contact_<unit>`, so degrees and millimetres never share a key; the report carries `step_degrees` and `step_mm`, null when undeclared. Each pair reports minimum distance, maximum common volume and first sampled contact; reports include timings, limits and explicit incomplete coverage. Acceptance remains advisory [rec: misty-spark-6372] [rec: curious-cedar-4881].

Endpoint-inclusive samples use at most the declared step, capped at 73 poses and 2,000 pairs per joint. Native-query budgets are 90 seconds per joint and 180 seconds per assembly; the enclosing script timeout still applies. Each joint verifies solved-pose agreement within 0.0001 mm and 0.001 mm³. A limited joint whose kind's step is undeclared, an open-ended limit, any other limited kind (cylindrical included), unsupported or unavailable geometry, flexible/closed/coupled/static-joint graphs, unsolved assemblies and exhausted budgets are reported `incomplete` with a reason, never skipped; unlimited joints are outside coverage. Known-angle fixtures: the hinge sphere fixture reports first contact at 79° against an analytic 78.522° within its 1° step; the slider two-sphere fixture reports first contact within one 0.75 mm step of −2 mm with the analytic lens volume at the nearest sample, and the same result with connector order reversed. Published results and accepted identity survive a cadexd restart in the staged payload for both kinds [rec: misty-spark-6372] [rec: curious-cedar-4881].

**The published sweep reaches the agent and the CLI (ADR-350).** The `clearance` inspect scope carries the published side table with pair names, extrema, first-contact values and timings through the existing paged JSON Pointer contract (limit 50); `cadex clearance --sweep` writes `docs/clearance-sweep.md` with the accepted revision, coverage and the unchanged measurements, exits 0 whenever the report is written, and reports a missing legacy sweep as explicitly unavailable without touching `script.json` or accepted result files. Complete coverage is explicitly not a fit verdict, and static thresholds do not reinterpret the sweep [rec: green-river-3790].

**The published sweep now reaches the agent in the build reply itself, not only through `inspect scope=clearance path=/clearance_sweep` (ADR-366) [rec: mellow-sky-2112].** `cadex_cli.clearance.sweep_summary` folds a `sweep` block into the `fit_summary` every successful `write_script`, `edit_script`, `set_params` and `rebuild` reply already carried, read from the same published value — no second engine call, no second measurement. It summarises per limited joint exactly the three facts F3 asks for: minimum distance, maximum common volume and the joint value of first contact with the pair that reached it, plus coverage and every pair that interpenetrates anywhere in a range. Failing means overlap *through the motion* — a maximum common volume above the same threshold the static block uses — plus any pair the engine could not measure; distance is reported, not judged, because a declared contact sits at 0 mm through a whole range and a distance rule would fail every attached horn. `pass` only when every limited joint was swept to completion with no overlap; `incomplete` carries the per-joint reason; `unavailable` is the F5 case, carrying the engine's declare-the-steps reason. Advisory throughout: reported, never refused. Measured on the shape that motivated it — the same hinge built with and without `sweep_step_degrees`, where the static verdict passes either way — the reply is the difference between `sweep pass: 1 joint swept, minimum distance 16 mm` and `sweep unavailable` with the reason. `cli/tests` 783 passed, 1 skipped; six new tests red on the previous code [rec: mellow-sky-2112].

**Known gap, named and deliberately not taken [rec: mellow-sky-2112].** The engine skips `_measure_joint_sweeps` entirely when neither step is declared, so nothing enumerates the limited joints that went unswept: the block says `unavailable` with the engine's reason rather than counting them. Emitting an `incomplete` row per limited joint in that case is an engine change with ADR-027 goldens behind it and is a separate unit, worth taking only if a design turn shows the reason alone is not enough. F5's create turn is the measurement of why it matters: it accepted an arm whose two revolute joints declared limits (±90°, ±100°) and whose assembly declared no `sweep_step_degrees`, so the engine ran no sweep and the agent found the gap only one continuation later [rec: flat-cove-2253] [rec: easy-otter-0439].
