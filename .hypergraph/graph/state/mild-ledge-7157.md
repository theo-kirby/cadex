Three amended rules bind every role [rec: keen-wing-6569]: **a usage limit is not an attempt** — void, receipt kept, retried with the same frozen prompt in a fresh suffixed project or seed copy, reported apart from the design's attempts; **design turns wait for the product agent** — it shares the actor's Claude account, so a dispatch happens only while that harness is available and never from a fallback harness, and after a void call the actor moves to tooling, tests or reconcile; **no role stops, starts or restarts the run**. The restart's rungs: R1 (done) and R2 — with Claude available, the F4 repair on a fresh seed copy (done, exhausted), then F5 (done, exhausted on `ot7-heron-c` with one count failing), then F6 and F7 in fresh suffixed projects through their continuations as each fit report requires [rec: keen-wing-6569] [rec: narrow-wave-7452] [rec: damp-cedar-5823] [rec: sunny-chart-5873]. A fourth rule joins them from the 2026-09-17 directive: **an unchanged refusal is not new evidence** — the wait is not filled with substitute units, repeated waiting records or repeated probes, and F6/F7 resume only on a *successful* availability check for the product agent's own model [rec: fair-badger-6443]. That check succeeded on 2026-09-17: the owner restored Fable access, the resume directive superseded the wait, and F6's create turn has completed on `ot7-robin-c` — the fourth rule's waiting posture now applies only if access is lost again [rec: modest-banner-8771] [rec: gentle-sand-0141] [rec: modest-dune-5265].

**Owner-directed work for run `ot7` (ADR-341): make the product agent design mechanisms that fit, on its own [rec: kind-dusk-1609].** It replaces the ot6 charter (ADR-328, `round-sun-8398`), whose D1, D2 and D4–D10 the owner ticked on 2026-09-14; D3, the rendered look, went to the owner's own manual work and does not carry [rec: nimble-wing-3050]. The measurement it is sized from: ot6 produced three buildable designs, but the product agent did not make them fit by itself — Finch was written by the actor with no model turn, the actor edited Robin's script twice, and Heron's three defects (a floor plane in the design, 248.2 mm³ of servo tab buried in its cheek, a horn left 0.2 mm from its link) were found by a hand-run probe and fed back as hand-written turns while the agent's printed output said the parts fit each time. The cause is in the product, not the model: the engine already measures every part pair's gap and overlap at the solved pose (`_measure_clearance` in `src/Mod/cadex/cadex_assembly_worker.py`), but the agent's tools could not reach those measurements, its reply carried the script's stdout, its system prompt told it to verify by printing, and nothing checked fit across a joint's range [rec: kind-dusk-1609].

In priority order [rec: kind-dusk-1609]:

1. **Measured fit in front of the agent.** After every design turn that builds, the reply and tools carry measured clearances and overlaps from the published result; the instructions say a printout is a claim and the measurements are the evidence [rec: kind-dusk-1609].
2. **A design says what should touch.** Declared intended contacts and clearances between named parts, checked against the geometry. A failing fit is reported, never refused: the design still builds and accepts, and existing projects keep opening.
3. **Fit through the motion.** Every joint with declared limits is swept through its range, first contact reported at the value where it happens.
4. **Proof the agent uses it, unassisted.** The arm, balancer and biped prompts, in fresh projects, reach designs with no failing fit, no actor edit and no human design feedback, each passing a short smoke rollout. A design that does not get there is a valid measured result.

The owner's fixed choices, not open to actor interpretation [rec: kind-dusk-1609] [rec: keen-wing-6569]: **the actor never edits a design** — in any `ot7-*` project every change to the script, its parameters or its accepted state comes from a product-agent turn, and when the agent cannot run the actor records the refusal and works on tools, checks or tests; **prompts are frozen** — create prompts and at most three continuation prompts per design are committed under `docs/probes/ot7/prompts/` before the first design turn, a continuation prompt names no part, number or defect, a changed prompt starts a new attempt, and every attempt is reported; **failing fit is reported, never refused**; **the dashboard and the look are the owner's** — no edits to `cli/cadex_cli/review_static/`, `docs/REVIEW-DESIGN.md`, `docs/review-design/` or the video style, and the operator dashboard service is left alone; **no policy training** — smoke rollouts only, each bounded to five minutes; new test projects live outside the checkout under the operator's cadex-projects directory as `ot7-*`, and the ot6 projects are read-only, worked on as copies. Out of the frontier: printability rules beyond F2, a prompt benchmark beyond the three designs, reward design, print-ready export, catalog expansion beyond what a design needs, inherited-tree removals. Standing rules carry from ot6: committed receipts at most 16 KB and images 200 KB, no secrets, machine-specific absolute paths, private addresses or hostnames; exhaustion policy `report_done`; a reconcile pass every five work iterations or three unreconciled records, with the separate maintainer and planner off and the critic naming the next unit.

The ten done criteria F1–F10 are child state nodes: F1 `wild-horizon-5461`, F2 `winter-key-1482`, F3 `curious-quill-9036`, F4 `polished-forest-0215`, F5 `stormy-aspen-5433`, F6 `narrow-dune-9454`, F7 `rapid-grove-9687`, F8 `honest-ivy-8824`, F9 `eager-summit-3153`, F10 `first-snow-5587`. ## Negative knowledge

- [scope: a `cadex smoke` `support: pass` as evidence an ot7 design stood, on a receipt written before ADR-377 | confidence: high | evidence: humble-harvest-9420] It only means a base geom touched the floor and stopped moving, which a toppled design satisfies as it lands. Two things follow for the F5–F7 bar, which each end on "a passing smoke rollout": such receipts do not distinguish standing from fallen, and **a two-wheeled balancer given no torque falls over in under half a second**, so F6 needs a design that stands unaided. Since ADR-377 the check reads the base's attitude against its accepted keyframe and reports the topple.

- [scope: product-agent design turns in run ot7 while the actor's Claude window is spent | confidence: high | evidence: blue-slope-0916, slender-spring-1027, keen-wing-6569 | decision: keen-wing-6569] Dispatching from a fallback harness yields only void receipts: the product agent shares the actor's Claude account, so all six calls refused in two to four seconds with no model turn. The amended charter (ADR-355) makes waiting for the harness a rule and voids such calls rather than counting them as attempts.
- [scope: the ot7 runner's `void_reason` classifier | confidence: high | evidence: vast-river-7582] A `<synthetic>` assistant frame is not limit evidence by its model name alone: an `authentication_failed` frame carries the same name and is an ordinary provider failure. Only an `error: rate_limit` tag, limit text, a rejected `rate_limit_event` or a 429 result voids a call.
- [scope: the F4 repair prompt on `ot7-heron-repair-b` under the CLI's then-unbounded thinking and the runner's 30-minute turn bound | confidence: medium | evidence: rare-birch-0755, sunny-chart-3499, stormy-snow-5452] After reading the measured fit, the model spent one 64,000-token message almost entirely on thinking, hit the output cap without a tool call, and never submitted before the runner killed it. With ADR-356's per-message cap the next turn on d completed in 1,461.9 s with no message at the cap; the wall was the unbounded message, not the prompt. The F5 create prompt at `high` then walled the same way across three capped messages, and completed at `medium` [rec: mellow-summit-9733] [rec: flat-cove-2253]; see the entry below.
- [scope: reading Claude's availability from a one-word probe before a frozen dispatch | confidence: high | evidence: stormy-snow-5452, terse-mesa-7963, plain-dusk-2906, forest-bell-5161, restless-gate-7062, sunny-chart-5873, idle-crow-9434, mellow-sky-2112] A probe that answers proves nothing about room: c was dispatched at 95 % and cut off at 101 %. The utilization is what matters; since ADR-358 the runner reads it itself before every frozen prompt and defers without room, and an editing turn needs about 50–55 points, so dispatch under about 45 %. The runner's bound reads the five-hour window only; the seven-day window read 90–94 % `allowed_warning` in the `continue-2` stream and is not gated, so a seven-day trip arrives as a void call rather than a deferral. An owner-side account refresh (ADR-361) resets both readings: after it the five-hour reset moved from 17:20 to 18:50 UTC and the seven-day window read 0–4 % [rec: restless-gate-7062] [rec: sunny-chart-5873]. **And a low window is not availability at all**: since ADR-364 the gate reads the probe's own outcome first, because three probes were refused at the organisation level while the five-hour window read 1–4 % — a number-only gate would have dispatched every one of F6's four frozen prompts into a refusing model [rec: idle-crow-9434] [rec: mellow-sky-2112]. **And before ADR-369 the refusal receipt could name the wrong limit**, reading whichever `rate_limit_event` frame the provider sent first; a refused probe now reads the frame that rejected [rec: mellow-marsh-0749].
- [scope: actor iterations spent waiting for the product agent's five-hour window in run ot7 | confidence: high | evidence: forest-lantern-9096, forest-bell-5161, neat-reef-5625] Every actor iteration before a reset spends the same window the product agent needs and cannot lower it; waiting iterations should probe, dispatch nothing and stop, and an iteration that dispatches nothing and changes nothing needs no record.
- [scope: a frozen continuation prompt that asserts failing checks remain, on a design whose product checks all pass | confidence: high | evidence: plain-dusk-2906, damp-cedar-5823] The assertion does not move the agent: it re-reads the measurements, re-accepts the unchanged script and records a decision overruling the prompt. Under the charter's rule that a continuation names no part, number or defect, a continuation cannot steer the agent toward a defect the product's checks do not name; only a check can.
- [scope: an ot7 create turn at effort `high` under the 32,000-token per-message cap and the 30-minute runner bound | confidence: medium | evidence: mellow-summit-9733, flat-cove-2253] The per-message cap does not bound a turn's thinking: the harness auto-resumes a capped message, so one long thought becomes several and the bound still falls. Effort is the control that fit: the same frozen prompt completed at `medium` in 1,530.4 s with no cap hit. One pair of runs.
- [scope: the product's fit report as the acceptance bar on an unassisted create turn | confidence: medium | evidence: flat-cove-2253] The agent repairs from the report once and then accepts intersections it can explain (thread engagement) and a world-marked bench as fit. F2 has no declared engagement, so a screw modelled in a tapped hole always fails the overlap check; whether the agent resolves that by retracting screws, as on `ot7-heron-repair-d`, or by explaining it is what the continuations measure, and the check-design question is reported, not decided.
- [scope: the product agent's design turns on a build reply that carries fit but not inventory, the product before ADR-362 | confidence: high | evidence: flat-cove-2253, easy-otter-0439, forest-bell-5161, sunny-chart-5873 | decision: lawful-grotto-1291] Catalog identity lost by modifying a `lib.*` part is invisible to the agent: across four F5 turns it never read the inventory scope and asserted in every closing message that the servos and horns were catalog hardware while the published inventory listed them uncatalogued.
