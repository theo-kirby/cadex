## Spine and feet (`spine_foot.py`, new for leo)

- **Spine.**
  - Both joints sit bent towards their negative stop the whole episode,
    from −14° to −25° each (−29° to −50° in all).
  - The share of frames within 1° of a stop is 0.01 (largest 0.04).
  - With almost no strides, the range per stride is undefined.
  - **The cable holds.** The largest `|j_spine2 − j_spine|` on any seed is
    0.19°, under O9's 1°. judge.py's closure row `coupling/cable_spine`
    gives a median of 0.009° and a maximum of 0.19°.
- **Rigid foot**, medians over seeds:

| leg | heel share of stance (engine) | heel-down frames flat | toes-up | sole tilt mm (toes − heel) | heel mm in stance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LH | 0.47 (0.47) | 0.30 | 0.70 | −1.4 | 1.2 |
| RH | 0.20 (0.20) | 0.14 | 0.86 | −4.5 | 4.4 |

- The heel share read here equals the engine's `heel_down_share` on both
  legs, which checks the reading.
- When the heel is down, the toe bulbs are off the floor in 70% and 86% of
  those frames. This standing policy rocks back onto its heels, which is
  orun12's toes-up fault on a rigid foot.
- In paw stance, the toes sit 1.4 mm and 4.5 mm below the heel.

## Real meshes (`docs/probes/orun12/trace_check.py`, unchanged on leo)

- **No pair** of parts that are apart in the build pose interpenetrates
  deeper than 5 mm.
- **The front rod loops are open past O9's 2 mm.** `j_frodbot_l` is 0.12 mm
  at the median and 2.42 mm at worst; `j_frodbot_r` is 0.12 mm and
  3.03 mm. The hind loops stay at 0.34 mm and 0.42 mm. This is the same
  front-rod gap as orun12's Q2 (2.71 mm and 2.86 mm on 910cdb5a), now on a
  standing policy.
- **The front paw meshes reach 17.7 mm and 18.0 mm below the floor**
  (`c_paw_fl`, `c_paw_fr`), while their collision capsules stand on it. The
  front paw's real mesh hangs about 18 mm below its capsules. On film the
  front paws will look sunk into the floor. Q2/F1 should check this before
  trusting a still. The hind paws are at −0.8 mm and −1.6 mm, and the heels
  at −2.2 mm and −1.8 mm.
- The mesh heel share (8 mm bar, 0.96 / 0.89) is not the engine's (1 mm
  capsule bar, 0.47 / 0.20). P1 is judged on the engine's.
