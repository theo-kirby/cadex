## Where RH's heel-down frames fall (sg-s5-25)

`sg-s5-25.md` left one candidate cause for RH's heel running against the
command: RH lands about 0.15 of a stride before the clip's touchdown, where
`ref_rh_down` is 0 and `heel_sched` pays nothing. `heel_gate.py` reads every
settled paw-down frame of sg-s5-25's judged traces with `hind_rhythm.py`'s
clip clock and `heel_sched.py`'s metatarsus pitch, and splits each hind's
heel-down and heel-up frames by the clip's flag: **in** its stance, **early**
(clip swing, nearer the next touchdown) or **late** (nearer the last lift).

(Pay is `heel_sched`'s per-step share for one leg, w 6, the mean over that
leg's paw-down frames of the split, the gate's zeros included; contrast is
heel-up minus heel-down, positive pushing the heel up.)

**Read.**
- **Yes, most of them.** RH's heel-down frames fall in its early contact,
  before the clip's touchdown: 0.63 at 1000, 0.68 at 1700, 0.96 at 2400.
  None fall late. LH's do at 1000 (all of its few) but sit in the clip's
  stance at 2400 (0.94).
- **That flips the term's sign on RH at speed.** At 2400, as built,
  `heel_sched` pays RH's heel-up frames +1.00 more than its heel-down ones,
  because the heel-down frames are almost all outside the gate (0.13 a
  step) while the heel-up frames are inside it. It pushes RH's heel **up**
  at the fast end, where it should pull it down; LH, landing on time, gets
  −1.37. That is RH's falling schedule (0.39 → 0.33 → 0.29), and why
  `heel_sched.py`, which used paw contact for the gate, scored both legs
  alike.
- **Gated on paw contact, the contrast has the schedule's sign on both legs
  at both ends:** +1.22 / +1.23 at 1000 (heel up), −1.47 / −1.35 at 2400
  (heel down), small and positive at 1700 (target +21°, between).

**The fix, scored and not built.** `heel_sched` with the paw's own contact
channel (`chl_down` / `chr_down`, the `c_paw_h*` contact observations already
in the bundle, privileged, as `hind_scuff` uses them) in place of the clip's
stance flag; one term, the same weight, nothing else changed:

```
(chl_down * S_l + chr_down * S_r) / 2      # was (ref_lh_down * S_l + ref_rh_down * S_r) / 2
S_k = sat01-style tent of _META[k] about T(cmd) = 6 + 30 (2400 - cmd) / 1400, half-width 30
```

In `orun16-sg6.py` that is one line, the `heel_sched` reward's
`"(ref_lh_down * %s + ref_rh_down * %s) / 2"` becoming
`"(chl_down * %s + chr_down * %s) / 2"`. A contact gate also pays during
LH's mid-swing taps (0.88 a clip stride at 1000 under sg-s5-25); those taps
are heel-up frames on the toes (LH's heel-down frames at 1000 are 0.07 of
its contact), so at 1000 they earn the slow end's heel-up pay, which
`hind_scuff` (w −8) charges back at up to 4 a step on the same frames.
