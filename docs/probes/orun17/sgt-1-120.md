## What 120 shows beyond the rule

Against sg-s9-80 and sgt-1-50:
- **It tracks the slow end.** 0.95 m/s at 0.7 (136 %), the closest any SG
  policy has held the slow command (sg-s9-80 1.07, sgt-1-50 1.64), and one
  hind rhythm at every command (2.00 / 2.44 / 3.00 Hz, both legs equal;
  sg-s9-80's slow end ran LH at 3.61 Hz against RH 2.00).
- **The heel came down.** Heel stance height 4–7 mm at every command
  (sgt-1-50: 11–39), and the engine heel share 0.26–0.35 at 1550 and 2400.
  It does not rise with speed on LH, and at 700 it is already 0.12 / 0.19,
  so the slow end is not digitigrade (on the pad, R 0.78–0.84, toes T
  ≤ 0.03): SG's slow clause fails the other way from sgt-1-50's toe-bulb RH.
- **The fold moved to both legs at 1550** (U 0.24 / 0.30) and stays on LH
  at 2400 (0.27). Every lift is toes-down of the floor (0.00 lifts toes-up
  on every leg and command), so O2's "the paw lifts flat" holds (floor
  pitch at lift −8.7…+1.5°, within 10°).
- **Lag.** The hind share falls with the command for the first time on an
  SG screen. The fores are rotary but 0.083 of the stride at 700, the
  slow-end fault since sg-s9-25.
- **LH still reaches** (overreach +53 / +186 at 1550 / 2400 against RH's
  −343 / −242); the mirror at 50 showed that is the policy's.
- **Spine** 28.6° a stride at 700 falling to 11.1° at 2400, never at a
  stop (orun7: ~60 % at the clamp); `j_spine` is the worst drive at 700
  and 2400 (RMS 1.56 / 1.62 of rated).
