# orun18 A1: Playground's Go1 joystick (flat), trained and judged

Verified against source: 2026-10-23. Frozen run evidence; read it, never cite
it as current.

## What ran

- **Recipe:** MuJoCo Playground 0.2.0, `Go1JoystickFlatTerrain`, its
  published `locomotion_params.brax_ppo_config(env)` unchanged, its Go1
  domain randomiser, seed 1. Launcher: `a1_train.py` (ours, Playground's
  public API; the repo's `learning/train_jax_ppo.py` is not in the wheel).
- **One deviation:** `impl="jax"` (MJX). Playground 0.2.0's Go1 default is
  `impl="warp"`, which needs `mujoco_warp` (not installed; loading fails
  without it). MJX is the backend Cadex's trainer uses, so it is the like
  for like choice for A2; a warp reference run is not part of A.
- **Versions:** see `SOURCES.md` — playground 0.2.0, brax 0.14.2, jax 0.7.2,
  mujoco / mujoco-mjx 3.10.0 (the same jax/brax/mjx pins as
  `~/cadex-train-venv`), Menagerie `1b86ece5`.
- **Machine:** the RTX 5090, alone (machine lock held through `flock` on
  `~/.cache/cadex/training.lock`), in `systemd-run --user --scope -p
  MemoryMax=30G`.
- **Outputs (outside the repo):** `~/orun18/a1/` — `params.pkl` sha256
  `34bb772a…c09e600`, `progress.jsonl`, `config.json`, `judge.json` sha256
  `31da8e3f…c63fa16`, `judge-beyond.json` sha256 `9ca6055c…bc25d`,
  `traces/` (qpos per seed and command, for F1's film).

## D3's judgement (`go1_judge.py`, stock MuJoCo on the CPU)

10 seeds (0–9) per command, 8 s from the home pose with a seeded
perturbation, lateral and yaw commanded 0, deterministic policy, noise off.
Speed is forward body-frame speed averaged over the last 2 s. Torque is
`actuator_force` over the actuator's forcerange (23.7 N·m hip/thigh,
35.55 N·m calf).

| command m/s | falls | last 2 s mean | min seed | best 2 s | lateral m/s | yaw rad/s | worst RMS / limit | worst peak / limit | frames at clamp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 0/10 | 0.486 | 0.479 | 0.496 | 0.031 | 0.010 | FR_calf 0.24 | FL_calf 0.52 | 0.000 |
| 1.0 | 0/10 | 1.008 | 1.002 | 1.021 | 0.011 | 0.020 | FR_calf 0.26 | FR_thigh 0.60 | 0.000 |
| 1.5 (top of the trained vx range) | 0/10 | 1.526 | 1.519 | 1.540 | 0.019 | 0.002 | FR_calf 0.28 | FR_thigh 0.74 | 0.000 |
| *2.0, beyond the range* | 0/10 | 2.013 | 2.005 | 2.030 | 0.029 | -0.000 | FL_calf 0.31 | FL_thigh 0.95 | 0.000 |
| *2.5, beyond the range* | 0/10 | 2.482 | 2.461 | 2.504 | 0.020 | -0.030 | FL_calf 0.35 | FR_thigh 1.00 | 0.001 |

Within 3% of command at every judged speed, no falls, no drive at its clamp
in range. The two rows past Playground's command range (vx ∈ [-1.5, 1.5])
are informational, for B2's Froude comparison; they are not part of A3's
yardstick.

The referee was checked first: a zero-action policy stands on 3/3 seeds
(drift < 0.003 m/s), and a 3 M-step smoke policy falls, so the judge tells
standing, walking and falling apart.
