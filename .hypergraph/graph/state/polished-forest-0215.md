**F4's residual is explained, and the product now measures it (ADR-370) [rec: honest-sky-8719].** The reason the agent stopped at two of three defects is in the product, not the model: on `ot7-heron-repair-d` the design welds each horn to its link with a `fixed` joint **and**, in the same `assembly.assembly` call, declares that pair a **clearance of 0.05 mm**. **ADR-379 is withdrawn (ADR-380), and F4's measured result is what it always was: zero failing checks, and a rebuild of `ot7-heron-repair-d` would report zero too [rec: terse-chart-0277].** ADR-372 had kept one escape hatch in its own words — "an explicit `contacts=` or `clearances=` declaration on the same pair still wins" — and ADR-379 read the floating horn's survival as proof that the hatch was a contradiction, making a `clearances=` declaration on a welded pair itself the failing check `clearance under weld` [rec: glad-wing-9845]. ADR-380 withdrew that: a fixed joint fixes a relative pose without requiring the solids to meet, and a declared minimum is a floor on a distance rather than a claim of motion, so a welded-and-spaced design is legitimate and the declared minimum is consulted as written (detail on `winter-key-1482`). **`glad-wing-9845`'s claim that a rebuild of `ot7-heron-repair-d` "would now report two failing pairs where it reported none" is withdrawn with it.** Its reading of F4's own numbers stands, and those numbers never moved under either decision: no retained receipt carries a clearance intent with `joints`, so no count in the closing report changes. **What is true about F4's defect, plainly:** the floating horn is real and unrepaired — `weld_horn_shoulder` holds `comp_horn_shoulder` 0.2 mm off `comp_upper_arm` with nothing spanning it — and it is **not** a failing fit check. What names it is `fit.attachments`, `not touching`, with its joint, advisory by ADR-370's deliberate choice. Whether an unspanned weld gap should become a check of its own is **open and deliberately undecided**: it needs a way for a design to declare the spanner, a standoff or a captive fastener, and inventing one inside a correction would repeat ADR-379's mistake. The agent's prompt now tells it to read `fit.attachments` and treat a `not touching` weld as a connection the geometry does not make, which is the reversible half [rec: terse-chart-0277]. F4 and F5 were measured under the ADR-372 rule that ADR-380 restored, so F4, F5, F6 and F7 are all judged under one rule after all [rec: terse-chart-0277].

- [scope: the F4 repair prompt on `ot7-heron-repair-b` under the CLI's then-unbounded thinking and the runner's 30-minute turn bound | confidence: medium | evidence: rare-birch-0755, sunny-chart-3499] The model reads the measured fit but then thinks to the output cap without submitting; the turn ends on the runner's clock, not the model's. ADR-356 bounds every message since; on d no message reached the cap.
- [scope: the product's declared-intent checker (F2) on a horn welded to its link and declared a clearance | confidence: high | evidence: stormy-snow-5452, damp-cedar-5823, honest-sky-8719, glad-wing-9845] A horn 0.2 mm from the link it drives is never a failing row: 0.2 mm clears the 0.1 mm default, and the design declared that exact pair a 0.05 mm clearance while *also* welding it with a `fixed` joint, so all four checks pass honestly on four turns. The contradiction — welded and cleared at once — was visible to nothing in the product until ADR-370, and to only a hard-coded pair list in the collector. It was measured and reported as `not touching` by ADR-370 and deliberately not made a failure then. ADR-379 made it one for a day, on the argument that declaring a running clearance on a welded pair is a contradiction; **ADR-380 withdrew that** — a fixed joint does not require touching and a declared minimum does not imply motion, so the declaration is legitimate — and the pair is once again no failing row of any kind. Naming it needs a way to declare the spanner, which no decision has yet taken [rec: glad-wing-9845] [rec: terse-chart-0277].
- [scope: a frozen continuation prompt that asserts failing checks remain, on a design whose product checks all pass | confidence: high | evidence: plain-dusk-2906, damp-cedar-5823] The assertion does not move the agent: it re-reads the measurements, finds nothing failing, re-accepts the unchanged script and records a decision overruling the prompt. A continuation cannot steer the agent toward a defect the checks do not name without naming it, and the charter forbids naming it.
- [scope: dispatching a frozen prompt after a one-word probe answers | confidence: high | evidence: stormy-snow-5452] An answering probe proves nothing about room in the window: `ot7-heron-repair-c` was dispatched at 95 % and cut off at 101 %. The first `rate_limit_event` frame of a turn carries the utilization; since ADR-358 the runner's own probe reads the utilization before dispatching.
