## Negative knowledge

- [scope: an ot7 attempt that is `paused` with continuations unspent | confidence: high | evidence: happy-ocean-5297, pale-dew-4616] Its silence about smoke is not a smoke pass. The runner attempts exactly one *closing* smoke, when an attempt closes; a paused attempt has no closing smoke receipt of any kind, and missing evidence is never a passing result — F6's precedent says the closing smoke is where a free-base design's real answer arrives. Since ADR-392 the silence is at least breakable without a slot: `run.py smoke` takes the same bounded rollout on an open attempt, which is how F7's was measured.
- [scope: reading a `cadex smoke` result on a retained attempt after an engine fix | confidence: high | evidence: glad-chart-3979, weathered-pebble-8544] It measures the **accepted revision's retained artifacts** and never rebuilds them, so an engine fix does not reach an old attempt — `ot7-plover-e`'s retry read the same pre-fix `plover_model-model.xml` and failed the same gate while a freshly built plover passed. A historical view is a view of history; closing such a smoke costs a turn that re-accepts the design, and that is a design slot.
- [scope: a dynamics-half `pass` on an ot7 smoke | confidence: high | evidence: pale-dew-4616] It is a pass on the model that was exported, not on the design. `ot7-plover-e`'s dynamics half passed every check while 24 of its 29 bodies — four carrying collision geoms, all 24 carrying mass — sat at the wrong world pose. The exact-BREP half's frame-0 agreement gate is what caught it, so read the two halves together and treat a half with no verdict as evidence withheld rather than evidence passed.
- [scope: editing engine source in this repository while an ot7 design turn is live | confidence: high | evidence: pale-wolf-9928, weathered-fountain-7838] It can destroy the turn without failing the runner. The iteration-158 ADR-389 edit republished the project-worker bundle 58 ms before the first `DOMAIN_WORKER_NO_RESULT` frame, so `ot7-plover-d` ran to a clean exit 0 / `success` with an accepted three-solid probe and no biped at all, its fit `unavailable`. A turn that ends on its own is not thereby a measurement of the agent; check what the repository did under it before reading the result, and rule such a call void.
- [scope: a create turn on a design of Plover's size | confidence: high | evidence: strong-hollow-7483, cool-ember-4875] A 1,800 s bound is **not** enough, and this is now settled rather than inferred: the three earlier create turns scaled with static-pair count (1,530.4 s at 120, 1,676.4 s at 276, 1,800.0 s at 435, the last being the bound itself), and the Plover create turn that succeeded under ADR-388's doubled bound took 2,695.8 s.
- [scope: the 13/435 static and 52 swept failures on `ot7-plover-c` | confidence: high | evidence: strong-hollow-7483] These are **not** an F7 result and must not be read as one. They describe an unaccepted candidate mid-repair; they are evidence for F1–F3 and for nothing else.
- [scope: reading F7's provider and collector failures | confidence: high | evidence: idle-crow-9434, mellow-sky-2112, soft-journey-2954, mellow-marsh-0749, modest-dune-5265] `narrow-dune-9454`'s entries apply to F7 unchanged: the five-hour window was not the binding limit during the 2026-09-16 refusal, the 2026-09-18T14:00:00Z epoch was evidence about the org-level refusal in neither direction, a pre-ADR-369 refusal receipt may name the wrong limit, and a collector whose parent session dies loses its turn as an interruption.
