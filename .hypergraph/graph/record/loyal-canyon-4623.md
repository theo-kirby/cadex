## What

The free-base engine unit that D6 was blocked on (ADR-335). An assembly that grounds nothing is no longer refused by either half: `assembly.solve` holds the first component in script order for the native solver (a grounded joint the script did not write) and reports it as `free_base` beside `grounded_components: []`, the `-6 no_grounded_component` verdict is gone; `extract_tree` gives that same component a free joint through the island path it already had; and `build_model` writes the environment's floor — one infinite plane `environment/floor` on the world body at z = 0 with MuJoCo's default contact parameters — when and only when nothing is grounded, recorded in the manifest as `dynamics.environment.floor` (a grounded model carries `environment: null` and gets no floor). Only `require_solved=True` holds a base, so no placement an accepted project could already carry moves. Finch is re-accepted without `grounded=True` (revision `bcce40a82d57…`, `free_base: pelvis_link`, 30 bodies, `nq` 11, 21 geoms = 20 declared boxes plus the world's floor), `docs/probes/ot6/finch/free_base_probe.py` steps the accepted export with stock MuJoCo (held at its zero targets it stands 2 s at 119.85 mm; shoved forward at 0.3 m/s with actuation disabled it falls onto the floor and rests with the pelvis at 30.26 mm, never below it; 2.79 mm transient impact penetration; eight checks hold in `free_base.json`), and `fit_check.py` regenerated the inventory and fit at the new revision with two rules added for the floor (87 checks, all hold; the proxy-contact rule now excludes `world`). The persistent operator URL serves the new revision (29 components, 20 proxies — the floor is on `<worldbody>` and is not a proxy).

## Why

The critic named the unit: "Unblock D6 next: implement the smallest free-base solve/export change that lets Finch fall while keeping world geometry outside its design. Preserve grounded assemblies and accepted-state checks. Verify a real ungrounded Finch export and short physics rollout, with an environment floor, before training." D6 (`dusty-otter-7562`) is the only blocked frontier node and the highest-ranked open criterion with D5's evidence complete; D7 and D8 need the same engine change. The critic's first item — "the plan still prioritizes old clearance work although no planner runs; supersede those priorities through the supported plan workflow" — is done the way the precedent records did it (`amber-gate-7498`, `ancient-key-7299`): a `plan/young-crane-9546` impact on this record that replaces the retired clearance/section/scaffold ranking with the ot6 ladder, for the next reconcile to fold; no generated view was hand-edited. Two routine choices resolved without a question, both reversible: (1) the free base is implicit (nothing grounded means free base) rather than a `ground=` argument on `assembly.mjcf` or the task — a script that grounds nothing has already said what it means, and ADR-335 records the alternative; (2) the floor's contact parameters are MuJoCo's defaults, stated in the manifest, because the pair rules already let a sole's declared friction win. One deviation from a literal reading of "lets Finch fall": passively, on straight legs, Finch does not fall — the pose is a balanced equilibrium a symmetric integrator never leaves — so the probe's falling evidence is the shoved rollout, and the passive one is recorded as measured.
