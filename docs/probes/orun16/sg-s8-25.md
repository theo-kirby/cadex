## sg-s9: cold on a 700–2400 schedule

`~/orun16-queue/orun16-sg9.py` differs from `orun16-sg8.py` in three
places only:
- `SG_STRIDES` gains the 0.35 m × 2.0 Hz stride (lag 0.35, heels up)
  ahead of sg0, and `SG_LOW` is 700 (the task's and `sg_spec`'s command
  range is 700–2400; `_SG_GATE` follows `SG_LOW`);
- `heel_sched`'s target spans the new range,
  `6 + 30 × (2400 − cmd) / 1700` (36° toes-down of the floor at 700, the
  +6° flat at 2400);
- `hock_land` is dropped.

Everything else is sg-s7's task (24 terms: the gallop task's, tent
`track_speed`, `overrun`, `hind_scuff` w −8, contact-gated `heel_sched`).
**Cold** (the reference and goal moved), seed 1, 4096 envs, 80 iterations
(sg-s2, the last cold SG screen, needed 80 to track the command), a
checkpoint every 25, `budget_s` 7,800 (80 × ~70 s + 20 % + compile).
Built with `cadex script --set` behind sg-s8 (reward and reference only;
the model is unchanged, so no clearance pair can move; no tag).

**The question:** trained cold from a 0.7 m/s slowest command, with no
warm habit to unlearn, does the slow end stand digitigrade on both hind
legs while the gait stays rotary?

**Judged at** 700 / 1550 / 2400 (`sg_judge.py --cmd 700 1550 2400`), with
`legs.py`, `paw_floor.py`, `p2_lag.py`, `hind_rhythm.py`.

**Rule at 25:** a cold screen's 25 is early (sg-s2 stepped in place at 25),
so stop only if a command falls on more than 2 of 10 seeds, or 2400's
median top 2 s speed is under 1.5 m/s while 700's is over 1.2 m/s (it
runs one gait at every command).

**Rule at 50:** run on to 80 only if
- on **both** hind legs the heel share at 700 is **at most 0.12**;
- rotary on at least 8/10 at 700 (all strides, taps counted);
- 2400's median top 2 s speed is at least 2.0 m/s.

Otherwise stop it as a dead end for its question and record which clause
failed.

**Rule at 80:** SG moves if both legs' heel share rises from 700 to 2400 by
at least 0.15 with 700 at most 0.1; RL's direction holds if at every
command rotary is 8/10, each pair's lag at least 40 ms with taps counted,
and the fore and hind lag shares fall from 700 to 2400. Extend warm (O8)
only if SG's holds.
