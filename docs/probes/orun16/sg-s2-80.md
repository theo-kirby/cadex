**Choice: W 10, B 0.25.**
- A gallop at its command pays the tent about 0.8 of its weight per step
  (v05-s1-50 at 2400: 15.9 of 20, the in-stride speed swing); the penalty
  takes 0.23 of that (1.5%), firing on 0.16 of steps.
- On sg-s2-50 at 1000 (2.0 m/s) it takes the tent's 7.66 to −0.56: the
  margin of running at the command over overrunning it goes from about 8
  to about 16 per step.
- It keeps overrunning above stepping in place (−0.56 against sg-s1's
  −1.77). W 20 does not (−8.79): a policy that cannot yet find the slow
  gallop would then be paid to stand, sg-s1's trap.
- On sg-s2-80 it costs 3.4 / 2.7 / 1.2 per step at 1000 / 1700 / 2400.

**The term**, checked by the engine's own formula checker
(`cadex_assembly_api._checked_formula`, 512-node cap), the 23rd term:

```
overrun (w -10) = (o + |o|) / 2,  o = (comv_x - 1.25 * cmd) / cmd
```

It is written into `~/orun16-queue/orun16-sg4.py` (sg-s3's script plus
these six lines; it differs from sg-s3's in this term only) and **not
built**: a build now would move the accepted revision from `e732b1dd`,
under sg-s3 while it trains. It is built when sg-s3 has been judged.
