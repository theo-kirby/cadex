- [scope: reading a retained ot7 fit receipt as what today's checker would say | confidence: high | evidence: pale-jasper-8166, loyal-flame-8896, wild-eagle-4128] A retained row is the measurement the accepting engine published. It carries no intent and reading it back recomputes none, so a later checker rule cannot move a retained number — the 44 / 39 / 20 comparison is stable by construction rather than by luck. The counterfactual beside it models ADR-372's weld exemption **alone**, on one retained measurement set, and is not a fresh-build verdict for Finch, Robin or Heron: ADR-370's attachment report, ADR-371's sweep coverage, ADR-373's clearance rule and ADR-374's swept roll-up are outside it, and the sharpest of those exclusions has its own measurement (ten of Robin's twenty-one welds hold nothing, at 0.3 and 0.6 mm).

- [scope: "existing projects keep opening" as a regression check | confidence: high | evidence: fresh-dawn-0892, frosty-sea-6051] It can break on an engine change that touches no BREP and no design, because a digest that hashes a *derived* output's artifact bytes cannot tell a better export from a changed model.
