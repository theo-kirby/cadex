The trace frames (seed 1003, 1.0–3.0 s) place it: every LH stance


LH lands heel-first with the paw 16° toes-up of the floor (pin +17.9° in
stance), and the pad's load takes 40–60 ms to lay it flat; RH lands flat
(F) on every stance. At lift both paws leave flat (−7.7° / −2.1°, 0.00

reading (a heavier damper resists the pad laying the paw flat), but one
seed does not separate it from the policy: hk12-s1-50's LH was the leg
with the fold too (0.16), in another form.
