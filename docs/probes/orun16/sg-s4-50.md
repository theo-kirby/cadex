## Why the hinds at 1.0 m/s run on two rhythms

The critic's question: LH 3.33 Hz against RH 2.11 at sg-s4-25 (4.00 against
2.22 at 50) under a 2.0 Hz clip. `hind_rhythm.py` (new) recovers the clip's
clock at the held command by the bundle's `tracking.schedule` rule, which
gives the blend weight, the blended clock and the origin fitted on that
stride's `reset_qpos`. It reads each paw's contact by the evaluator's stance
rule. Each touchdown is binned by its phase after the clip's own touchdown
of that paw (0 on time, 9 a tenth early), with its contact time:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| sg-s3-25-1000 | 1.37 / 1.08 | 6: 0.25 (20), 9: 0.74 (160) | 8: 0.89 (180), 9: 0.11 (160) | 0.37 / 0.09 | 0.320 / 0.320 |

**What it is.** At 1.0 m/s LH lands once a stride where the clip asks
(0.05 of a stride early, 140 ms of contact). Between landings it **taps the
floor for one or two frames (20–40 ms) at 0.5–0.7 of its swing**, where the
clip has the paw 30–40 mm up (`ref_lh_lift` peaks at 40 mm at 0.6). The
tap falls just after RH's landing (RH lands 0.15 of a stride early), so
`p2_lag` pairs RH's landing with LH's tap. That makes the "hinds together"
reading (31–52 ms) and `legs.py`'s 3.3–4.0 Hz. The tap rate grew with
training: 0.37 a stride at sg-s3-25, 0.69 at sg-s4-25, 0.99 at sg-s4-50.
That is the slower slow gait `overrun` asked for: a longer, lower swing on
the 0.5 m clip stride. RH taps rarely (0.10–0.17). At 1.7 and 2.4 m/s
neither paw taps. The two rhythms are LH's, the same left/right split as
every leo run (`orun15/hk12-mirror.md`: the policy's, not the hardware's).

**What it already costs.** `track_contact` (w 10 over four paws) charges
a tap 2.5 a frame, the same as an early landing. That is why it has not
stopped the tap.

### Two candidates, scored per step (O7)

Both use `fore_tap`'s weight (−8):
- `hind_tap`: `fore_tap`'s form on the hinds, `((1 - ref_lh_down) *
  chl_down + (1 - ref_rh_down) * chr_down) / 2`;
- `hind_scuff`: the same contact gated by the clip's swing height instead
  of its swing flag, `(chl_down * g(ref_lh_lift) + chr_down *
  g(ref_rh_lift)) / 2`, with `g = sat01((lift - 10) / 20)`.

**Read.**
- **`hind_tap` is cut before training**, as in orun15 (`hk12-mirror.md`).
  It charges the early landings on both legs at every command (−0.29 to
  −0.62 a step). It charges the tapping LH no more than the non-tapping RH.
- **`hind_scuff` separates them.** All of its charge falls on taps
  (0.99–1.00 of LH's). It grows with the tap rate (−0.07 → −0.12 → −0.25
  a step), and it is about zero at 1.7 and 2.4 m/s and on RH. Per tap it
  adds about 4 a frame to `track_contact`'s 2.5. That is about 0.25 of a
  ~39 reward a step, near `heel_sched`'s scale.
- Expression, for when it is built: `(chl_down * (((ref_lh_lift - 10) /
  20 + abs((ref_lh_lift - 10) / 20)) / 2 - ((ref_lh_lift - 30) / 20 +
  abs((ref_lh_lift - 30) / 20)) / 2) + chr_down * (…rh…)) / 2`, w −8. It
  adds one term (sg-s5 has 23, cap 32).

**Not built.** The critic asked for this to be scored, not built, while
sg-s5 trains. It is a candidate for sg-s5's successor if sg-s5's 25 judgement
finds the 1000 hind lag under 40 ms again.
