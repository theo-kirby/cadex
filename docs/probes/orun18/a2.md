# orun18 A2: the same Go1 through Cadex's trainer, and A3's verdict

Verified against source: 2026-10-23. Frozen run evidence; read it, never cite
it as current.

## The route, and why

**A task bundle written around Playground's own MJCF** (`a2_bundle.py`). It
is the smallest honest route: the model, its physics options and its contacts
are Playground's file, so A2 changes only what the trainer is being tested on,
the task and the learner loop. It is a port of orun6 B1's body-frame
generator (the bundle ADR-712's evidence trained), with Playground's domain
randomisation added where a bundle can say it. A Cadex project that rebuilt
the Go1 from Menagerie's numbers would also have tested the build, which is
not A's question.

- **Model check.** The bundle's `go1-model.xml` against Playground's compiled
  `env.mj_model` (`impl="jax"`), stepped together for 1000 steps from `home`
  under the same sinusoidal controls: **0.0 qpos drift**. Damping, armature,
  frictionloss, joint ranges, gains, biases, forceranges and every solver
  option match exactly, and so do the five contact geoms (friction, condim,
  priority). The model differs only by 4 massless foot frames, the sensors
  and no visual meshes (the total mass is 12.743 kg in both).
- **Bundle:** `~/orun18/a2/outputs/go1_joystick-task.json` sha256
  `6bc7869f…`, model `1d03aaeb…`. It has 87 channels, 60 of them the
  actor's, 12 reward terms and 39 randomisation entries. Nothing in it is
  committed: it embeds the BSD-3 Menagerie model.
- **The one thing outside the script language.** The actor reads the trunk's
  body-frame velocity (velocimeter), gyro and gravity (a framezaxis with a
  reference frame) as raw MJCF sensor rows. The trainer gathers these by
  address like any other sensor, but `cadex_assembly_api` cannot declare
  them yet. Playground's policy reads the same three.

## Training (cold, seed 1, alone on the RTX 5090)

`run1/launch.sh`: `training/cadex_train.py` from `~/cadex-train-venv`
(brax 0.14.2, jax 0.7.2, mujoco-mjx 3.10.0, the same pins as A1's venv), in a
`systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=30G` under the machine lock. It ran
with Playground's budget and hyperparameters: 1260 iterations × 8192 envs ×
20 steps, 4 epochs × 32 minibatches, 512-256-128 swish, γ 0.97, lr 3e-4,
entropy 0.01, clip 0.3, grad norm 1.0, `--reward-floor 0`, and a checkpoint
every 300 iterations.

| env steps to the final policy | 206,438,400 | **206,438,400** |

A1's 385 s includes its 10 evaluations; A2's 351 s includes 4 checkpoints,

0.018 (it 100) → 0.027 (400) → 0.029 (1260).

## D3's judgement (`go1_judge.py cadex`, the same referee, seeds and commands as A1)

The new `cadex` adapter plays the `.cxpolicy` the way the engine does: the
bundle's sensors at their addresses and scales, the goal channels, the
previous two issued commands, the normaliser, the MLP, then the output map
and the bundle's clamp × scale into ctrl. Before judging, it checks its
forward pass against the container's own 32 witness samples (largest error 1.2e-5,
float64 against float32). Outputs: `~/orun18/a2/run1/judge.json` sha256
`8e247ac6…`, `judge-beyond.json` `5468669…`, and `traces/`.

## A3: the verdict — **Cleared**

| command m/s | A1 last 2 s | A2 last 2 s | A2 / A1 | falls A1 / A2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5 | 0.486 | 0.477 | 0.982 | 0 / 0 |
| 1.0 | 1.008 | 0.973 | 0.965 | 0 / 0 |
| 1.5 | 1.526 | 1.436 | 0.941 | 0 / 0 |

A2 is within 10% of A1 at every judged command, with no more falls (0 of 30
each), in 0.91× A1's wall time (bar: 2×). The two rows beyond the range read
0.950 and 0.962 of A1, which A3 does not count. **Cadex's trainer is not why
leo trains worse than a stock quadruped.** On the same robot, task and
budget it learns the same skill as Playground's recipe, at the same cost.

What the verdict does not settle:
- **A2 under-tracks by 2–6%, and the shortfall grows with speed.** A1 is
  within 3% everywhere. The likeliest candidates, from the table below, are
  the missing `feet_air_time` / `feet_height` terms and the absent
  observation noise. Neither was tested alone here; A3 does not require it
  when cleared.
- **One training seed each.** Seed-to-seed spread is not measured on either
  side.
