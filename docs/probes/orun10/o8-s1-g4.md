# orun10 O8: what binds s1-g4's 4.49 m/s gallop, and the 3 Hz one

Verified against source: 2026-10-18. Frozen evidence (orun10, iteration 17).

O8 asks for a measurement of what binds before the next sprint lever. The
measurement covers stride length against frequency, flight, and which drive
is at peak in which part of the stride. It is taken on the two fastest
sprint policies judged so far:

- **s1-g4 final**, the 2.4 Hz clip: 4.486 m/s median and 4.498 best, the
  run's top speed (`s1-g4-judged.md`);
- **s1-g6 at 100**, the 3.0 Hz clip: 4.072 m/s median (`s1-g6-judged.md`).

The peak shares come from `~/orun10-progress/s2/o8_peak_phase.py` at 0.98
of each drive's clamp, as in `o8-peak-phase.md`. They are read on the
judged evaluations `orun10-cat/evaluations/s1-g4-final` and `s1-g6-000100`,
10 seeds each, 4,418 and 4,346 settled frames. The method is the engine's
`foot_series` on each run's exported model, with frames placed by the left
fore's touchdowns. Stride and flight figures are the judged ones from the
two reports. The run's numbers are in `~/orun10-progress/s14/o8-g4.out` and
`o8-g6.out`.

## Stride, frequency, flight

| policy | clip | top speed, median | cycle: length × freq | flight per cycle | footfall | worst RMS / rated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| s1-g4 final | 2.4 Hz | 4.486 m/s | 1.86 m × 2.38 Hz | 120 ms (two of 60 ms) | fl → fr, fl → hl → hr; fl strikes twice a cycle | 1.47–1.50 (j_elbow_l) |
| s1-g6 at 100 | 3.0 Hz | 4.072 m/s | 1.36 m × 3.00 Hz | 80 ms | fl → hl → hr → fr | 1.88 (j_spine) |

Both runs keep the clip's clock exactly: 2.38 and 3.00 Hz. The speed is
the clock times whatever cycle the policy reaches on it. From s1-g1 to
s1-g4 the cycle grew 1.77 → 1.86 m at a constant 2.38 Hz. Extending s1-g4
(s1-g5) only lost speed. Neither the weight of the clip's terms
(`reward-balance.md`) nor a longer clip (s1-g7) bought speed.

Every other drive is at peak in under 3% of frames on both. The hocks bound
orun7's f1-g3, at peak through their whole stance (0.18 and 0.24,
`o8-peak-phase.md`). They are not at peak on s1-g4.

## What it shows

- **At 2.4 Hz no leg drive and not the spine binds.** At 4.49 m/s the most
  saturated leg drive is the left elbow at the fl strike, 10% of frames.
  The spine never reaches its peak. The drives touch their clamp on every
  seed (peak margin 0), but only in brief spikes. Torque is not what holds
  s1-g4 at 1.86 m a cycle. What does hold it is the clip's 2.38 Hz clock:
  5 m/s there needs a 2.10 m cycle, 13% longer than any policy has reached.
- **At 3.0 Hz the spine binds.** It sits at its 22 N·m peak for 37% of all
  frames, through the flexion and extension around each flight. Its rated
  RMS goes to 1.88×. The cycle shrinks to 1.36 m, and the run collapses
  after 100.
- **So the clock is between the two.** s1-g7 called the clip lever spent
  after the 2.4 and 3.0 Hz clocks. This measurement says those two clocks
  bracket the spine's limit: it goes from 0 to 37% at peak between them,
  and nothing between them has been tried. At 2.7 Hz, 5 m/s needs a
  1.85 m cycle, which s1-g4 already runs at 2.38 Hz. The next sprint lever is the clip's clock
  at 2.7 Hz (`gallop_hz`, a cold run). It should be judged on this same
  table, to see whether the spine stays under its peak there.
- **The tail servos are the drives most at their clamp**, at 18–49% of
  frames on both runs. They are 1.9 N·m hobby servos that balance the
  tail, not propulsion. They are reported here (O3), and they are a drive
  limit to name in R1.
