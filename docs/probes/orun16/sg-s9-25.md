## The 25 rule, as written

"Stop only if a command falls on more than 2 of 10 seeds, or 2400's median
top 2 s speed is under 1.5 m/s while 700's is over 1.2 m/s."

- **Falls:** `no_fall` passes 10/10 at every command, so the first clause
  does not fire. In these docs "falls" is the `no_fall` predicate, kept
  apart from ending early (`sg-s8-25.md`: "no falls; completes ... 10/10";
  `sg-s2-25`'s 8 / 6 / 4 early ends did not stop it). The episodes still
  end early, **tipped past 30° on 8 / 7 / 0 of 10 seeds** at 700 / 1550 /
  2400, with the training episodes lengthening (124 → 206 steps over
  iterations 19–24). This is the measure the 50 rule's speed and rotary
  clauses will feel.
- **One gait at every command:** 2400 runs 1.15 m/s (under 1.5) but 700
  runs 1.08 (not over 1.2), so the second clause does not fire either. The
  policy does not yet follow the command (1.08 / 1.09 / 1.15 m/s).
