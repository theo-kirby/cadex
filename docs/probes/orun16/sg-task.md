## The SG task, built (leo rev `721d10da`)

`script.py` (project commit `19a99b9`, `cadex script --set
orun16-sg.py`), the only change a new task beside `gallop`:

- `GALLOP_PREDICATES` is `gallop_spec`'s list, named so `sg_spec` can
  share it. The gallop bundle is byte-equal to v05-s1's
  (`9140fb2e…`), and so is the model (`59bc5407…`): no running or judged
  policy moves, and no geometry changed (the build's sweep fails leo's
  baseline 12 pairs, all front leg against chest).
- `gallop_sg`: `reference=[sg0, sg1, sg2, sg3]`, each `assembly.gait` as
  `gallop_ref` (duty 0.2, swing 40 mm, spine 7°, `HEEL_CLIP`: body drop
  60 mm, pitch 6°, stance shift 120 mm, `flat_feet="sole"`,
  `trail_feet`) with the stride's length, rate, pair lag and
  `heel_from` from `sg-strides.json`.
- `goal("cmd", kind="speed", between=[1000, 2400], resample_seconds=3,
  ramp_seconds=1)`: three changes of command an episode, each ramped
  over a second.
- The reward is the gallop's 21 terms, with three changes:
  - `track_speed` (w 20) pays tracking, `UP * exp(-abs(comv_x - cmd) /
    (0.2 * cmd))`, not top speed;
  - `plantigrade`, `heel_pitch`, `hind_flat` and `fore_sync` are
    multiplied by the command's share of the range,
    `sat01((cmd - 1000) / 1400)`: nothing at 1.0 m/s, full at 2.4;
  - `paw_fold` (w −10) stays, as in the gallop task (`REPORT.md`).
- `sg_spec`: `gallop_spec`'s predicates with the command held at
  2,400 mm/s (`sequence=[2400]`). A judge at another speed swaps the
  sequence; the spec is outside the task's semantic digest.

## sg-s1, launched

`orun13/queue_run.py ~/cadex-projects/leo sg-s1 9000 80 … --expect-revision
721d10da… --seed 1 --task gallop_sg --envs 4096`, in its own
`systemd-run --user --scope -p MemoryMax=30G`; **launched 08:47:20**
(`~/orun16-queue/sg-s1.log`). Cold (a speed command is a new actor input),
80 iterations, a checkpoint every 25, `budget_s` 9,000 (80 × ~85 s + 20 %
+ compile).
**Timed:** the first iteration logged ~4.5 min after launch (compile), then
**69 s per iteration** (iterations 5 → 10: 581 → 927 s of trainer wall),
so 80 iterations end near 10:20, well inside the budget. Reward per step
14.5 at 0, 11.7–12.0 over 2–10 (a cold start on the tracking reward,
not comparable with the gallop task's).

**The question:** does the heel's stance share rise and the pair lag fall
with the commanded speed? **Rules, written before judging** (at 25, 50
and 75, each at commands 1000, 1700 and 2400 mm/s):

- stop at 25 only on a blow-up or a policy that falls on most seeds at
  every command;
- at 50: if the 2400 command's heel share is not above the 1000
  command's on either leg, or the 2400 command runs under 1.9 m/s, the
  screen has not learnt the schedule; stop it and change one lever (the
  gate, the tracking width, or the slow stride);
- extend past 80 (warm) only while the heel-share spread across the
  commands, the speed tracking and the curve are all still climbing.
